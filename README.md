# Prototypes, Products & Pitches

This repository is a portfolio of product concepts, interactive prototypes, venture theses, and analytics projects I have built across product management, investing, and data science. Each project below includes its original presentation.

## Projects

### FreshRoute AI

*NYU Product Management Club Case Competition*

Led 0-to-1 product definition for an IoT- and AI-powered freshness management platform for third-party food distributors. After interviewing five stakeholders across warehouse, delivery, inventory, and franchise operations, translated their pain points into product requirements and a Figma/Lovable MVP with five-minute sensor readings, live freshness scores, predictive spoilage alerts, and route recommendations. Presented the product and go-to-market strategy at the NYU PMC Case Competition, placing third out of 100 participants.

### Instagram Dailies

*Meta product prototype*

Conceived and prototyped with Codex a five-feature MVP that gives long-distance friends one asynchronous daily photo prompt inside an Instagram group chat. Translated three user pain points into a product objective and five-step journey, evaluated four solution directions, and scoped the MVP around daily prompts, camera responses, shared answers, reactions, and an archive. Presented the product strategy and interactive prototype to Meta Friends product and engineering leads, winning Best Prototype and Best Presentation.

### Contrarian Venture Thesis

*Space manufacturing and reusable heat shields · NYU Stern Entrepreneurial Exchange Group*

Developed a contrarian investment thesis on space manufacturing by mapping the low-Earth-orbit production value chain from launch and microgravity processing through product return. Researched companies across the sector, identified reentry thermal protection as a structural bottleneck, and argued for reusable heat shields that combine lightweight deployable structures, drag deceleration, and radiative heat dissipation, highlighting Space Forge and Stoke Space as representative approaches.

### Fermeate Startup Investment Pitch

*NYU Stern Entrepreneurial Exchange Group*

Built a startup investment pitch on Fermeate, a precision fermentation company using tunable, reversible light to control microbial gene expression during fermentation. Framed limited capacity and high expansion costs as the market problem, assessed a hardware-plus-machine-learning product that identifies optimal light patterns, modeled the addressable market and a potential strategic exit, and evaluated competitive, technical, and commercialization risks alongside opportunities in government grants and bioreactor partnerships.

### Apple Health BioSync Product Development

*Associate Product Manager · NYU Product Management Club*

Developed BioSync, a 0-to-1 feature concept for Apple Health that gives users a calendar-style view of their complete health history, including hourly breakdowns, custom date ranges, trend summaries, and configurable dashboards. Interviewed four users to identify problems with data accuracy, customization, interpretation, and visibility, then translated those findings into prioritized MVP features, an interactive prototype, success metrics, stakeholder planning, a release roadmap, and growth and revenue hypotheses.

### Aviation Disruption Prediction Models

*Data Analyst Project Manager · NYU Stern Business Analytics Club*

Analyzed historical plane-crash and fatality datasets to study aviation safety patterns, including relationships among people aboard, passenger fatalities, crew fatalities, and geography. Built a decision-tree analysis and tuned a random-forest model to predict fatality rate from passenger and crew counts. The reported model used 390 estimators, achieved an RMSE of 0.3434, and identified passenger count as the more influential feature.
